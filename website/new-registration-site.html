Our New Registration Site Is Live

Philadelphia Argentine Tango School has moved to a new registration system: **[register.philadelphiatangoschool.com](https://register.philadelphiatangoschool.com/)**. You'll use it to buy classes and packages, sign up for workshops, book private lessons, and check in. ### Your class cards and credit came with you If you had a class card, a private lesson package, a monthly pass or account credit in our old system, it has been moved to the new site. Nothing is lost. • **Class cards and lesson packages** show the number of classes or lessons you have left. • **Account credit** shows as your account balance. It's used automatically when you check out. • **Monthly passes** keep renewing on the same date, with the same card. You don't need to sign up again. ### Logging in for the first time There's no password to remember. 1. Click **Register** on our website (or go to **[register.philadelphiatangoschool.com](https://register.philadelphiatangoschool.com/)**), then click **Login** at the top of the page. 2. Enter **the email address you used with us before**. This matters: your cards and credit are attached to that email. 3. We'll email you a login link. Click it and you're in. If the site asks for your name and says "First time here", you've probably used a different email than the one we have on file. Try your other address, or write to us (below) and we'll sort it out. ### Please sign the liability release When you log in, you'll see a note asking you to sign our liability release. It takes about a minute and is good for a year. Please sign it before your next class, since you'll need it to check in. ### Check that everything is there On your account page, open **Your Cards & Passes** and make sure your class cards, private lesson packages or monthly pass look right. For your credit, open **Payment history & account credit** and look at your account balance. Most of this moved over automatically, so if anything looks wrong or missing, please tell me. I'd much rather hear about it now than have you find out at the front desk. ### Questions or problems? Contact me directly I'm handling all customer service during the changeover, and I'll fix anything that comes up. **Meredith Klein** — [help@philadelphiatangoschool.com](mailto:help@philadelphiatangoschool.com) Thank you for your patience while we make the switch. See you on the dance floor!
